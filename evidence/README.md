# Evidence Policy

This directory is reserved for **sanitized portfolio evidence**.

Suitable evidence may include:
- redacted OWASP ZAP screenshots,
- sanitized alert summaries,
- before-and-after aggregate findings,
- diagrams of the assessment workflow,
- examples of defensive remediation validation.

Do **not** publish:
- usernames or passwords,
- authentication/session tokens,
- cookies,
- private customer or company information,
- sensitive application URLs or internal endpoints,
- proprietary source code,
- raw authenticated scan exports,
- exploit-ready payloads or instructions.

The goal is to demonstrate security-assessment skills without increasing risk to the assessed application.
